How GeoTrack Uses Your Data

Your profile details keep your account recognizable across collections, interests, and support requests.

Your interests and saved places help GeoTrack rank recommendations that feel more relevant to the cities you browse.

Device location is used to improve nearby discovery. Precise GPS and approximate network-based coordinates are stored only for the current browser session, not long-term local storage.

If precise location is denied or unavailable, GeoTrack uses approximate network-based location automatically for nearby discovery. Clearing location state does not touch your account.
